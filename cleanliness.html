🧹 Clean & Green

A clean and green environment is important because it protects our health and supports all living things. Clean air and water help prevent illness, while healthy soil and plants provide food and homes for wildlife. Green spaces also make communities more pleasant and can reduce heat and pollution. By keeping our surroundings clean and caring for nature, we help people today and protect resources for future generations.

Cleanliness Tips:-

  • Wash your hands before eating.
  • Keep your kitchen clean.
  • Do not throw garbage on the road.
  • Separate different types of waste.
  • Keep drinking water clean.

💧 Healthy Daily Habits

Try to follow these habits every day:

  • 💧 Drink enough water as per your age and capacity
  • 🍎 Eat fruits to get essential vitamins and minerals
  • 🥦 Eat vegetables for fiber and nutrients
  • Be physically active eg. exercisefor at least 30 minutes a day
  • 🧼 Keep yourself clean and see your personal hygiene
  • Do not waste food use our website to calculate food waste
