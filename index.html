Welcome to Food2Future

Food2Future is a simple website that helps people eat healthy food and reduce food waste. It provides information about healthy eating habits, the importance of cleanliness, andthe impact of food waste on the environment. The website also includes a food waste calculator and a quiz to test your knowledge about sustainable development goals.

Small changes in our daily life can help us become healthier and protect our environment.

🌍 Sustainable Development Goals

Our website supports several United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 2 - Zero Hunger

Zero Hunger is one of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.It Encourages better understanding of nutritious food and reducing unnecessary food waste.

SDG 3 - Good Health and Well-being

WHO recommends a healthy diet to prevent malnutrition and reduce the risk of noncommunicable diseases. Food2Future Encourages healthy eating, physical activity and good hygiene.

SDG 6 - Clean Water and Sanitation

Water being the foundation of life, Food2Future Encourages cleanliness and responsible use of water.

SDG 12 - Responsible Consumption and Production

This is the main SDG of Food2Future. It focuses on responsible consumption and reducing waste.For a better future and helty well bein of the future generations.

SDG 13 - Climate Action

Reducing unnecessary food waste and making a proper waste segregation system at our home and food establishments can support more sustainable lifestyles.
