♻️ Food Waste

Food waste occurs when food is produced or bought in excess, stored poorly, or left uneaten because of confusing date labels, large portions, and changing plans. It can also happen during harvesting, transport, and retail when food is damaged, rejected for its appearance, or kept from reaching buyers.

How to Reduce Food Waste :

  • Cook only what you need.
  • Store leftover food properly.
  • Check your food before throwing it away.
  • Use older food first.
  • Reuse safe leftovers.
  • Recycle food packaging.
  • Compost fruit and vegetables scraps.
